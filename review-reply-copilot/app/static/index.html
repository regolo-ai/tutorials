CSV file

Expects: a UTF-8 CSV with a header row and one review per row.

  • textrequired, the review itself
  • ratingoptional, 1 to 5
  • titleoptional
  • authoroptional
  • dateoptional, any format

Also understood: review, body, content, comment (for text), stars, score, name, reviewer, created, published. Most review tools can export a CSV: check the export options of the one your client uses.
