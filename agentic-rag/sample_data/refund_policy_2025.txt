title: enterprise refund and returns policy 2025
version: 2025.1
date: january 15 2025
section: standard refund terms

the standard return window to request a full refund is thirty days from receipt of goods. all returned merchandise must remain in original packaging without signs of physical wear. the customer assumes direct shipping costs for returning non-defective merchandise to the regional fulfillment center.

section: refund exceptions and restrictions 2025

the following categories are strictly excluded from refund eligibility:
- digital software licenses once activated on external workstations.
- perishable goods and customized items manufactured to unique client specifications.
- hygienic goods unsealed after final delivery.
- merchandise damaged through improper handling or customer negligence.

section: credit settlement procedure

approved credits are processed within fourteen business days following physical receipt and verification at the central warehouse using the original payment method.
