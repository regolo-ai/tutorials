title: enterprise refund and returns policy 2026
version: 2026.1
date: january 10 2026
section: standard refund terms

the standard return window to request a full refund is extended to forty-five days from receipt for registered commercial accounts. all returned items must include original accessories and instructional documentation in unblemished condition. the enterprise covers all return shipping fees using prepaid courier logistics labels.

section: modified exceptions and policy variations 2026

the 2026 policy revision updates refund exceptions and introduces important modifications:
- merchandise damaged during transit is eligible for immediate full reimbursement upon notification within forty-eight hours.
- digital software licenses remain refundable within fourteen days if the registration code remains unredeemed on license servers.
- custom manufactured items remain non-refundable except for documented manufacturing defects verified by photographic evidence.

section: expedited credit settlement procedure

approved monetary reimbursements are issued through an accelerated schedule within five business days from the initial courier pickup scan.
